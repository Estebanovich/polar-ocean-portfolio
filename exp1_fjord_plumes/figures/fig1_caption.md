**Figure 1. Two-dimensional line plume against an ice face in MITgcm (configuration a) compared with line-plume theory.**
(a, b) Potential temperature (colour; values below 3.5 °C are shown dark) and vertical velocity (black contours at 0.1, 0.3 and 0.5 m s^-1, thin to thick) at t = 48 h for q0 = 0.03 m^2 s^-1 with a two-layer (a) and a Sermilik-like continuous (b) ambient. The ice face is at x = 0 and the horizontal grid is stretched (5 m at the face). (c, d) Plume velocity and (e, f) ice melt rate against depth, averaged over 36-48 h, for q0 = 0.01, 0.03 and 0.1 m^2 s^-1 (colours). Solid lines are MITgcm; dashed lines are the line-plume theory (Jenkins, 2011) started from the initial ambient profile and run with MITgcm's own melt law and linear ice-temperature profile. The solid velocity curve is the width-mean w of the plume, defined as the cells around the maximum with w at least 10 % of the maximum at that depth (edge interpolated between cell centres), compared with the theory's top-hat u. The dotted curve is w in the 5 m cell next to the ice; it is a post-hoc diagnostic and not part of the pre-registered tests.

*Pre-registered tests (theory in MITgcm melt-law mode, initial ambient).* C2 (detachment depth within 0.10 H): passes for 5 of 6 cases; sermilik q0 = 0.1 fails (difference 0.141 H). C2' (lower-half mean plume velocity within 25 %): fails in all six cases (model/theory 0.50-0.61) under the definition above. C2'' (mean face melt within a factor 1.5, each side averaged below its own detachment depth): passes in all six cases (ratio 1.00-1.23). *Post-hoc diagnostics* (defined after seeing the results; stored separately in fig1_metrics.json): the face-cell w is 0.86-1.05 of the theory u in the lower half, and its peak is 1.09-1.42 times the peak theory u (the near-wall core is about 10-40 % faster than the theory top-hat, increasing with q0); the momentum-weighted mean M/Q is 0.64-0.78 of the theory u; the model plume is two to three times wider than the theory's (about 1.5 cells at 5 m). The whole-face melt ratio is 1.07-1.50 (sermilik q0 = 0.1 sits at the factor-1.5 limit). Re-running the theory in the model's evolved ambient (window-mean T and S at x = 1 km) reduces the detachment-depth gaps (for example 0.141 H to 0.059 H for sermilik q0 = 0.1).

*Known model-theory differences* (class: a = our error, b = missing physics, c = numerical or set-up choice, d = reference error): (c) MITgcm drives the heat-transfer velocity with |w| of the 5 m face cell, the theory with the top-hat u; (c) MITgcm converts potential to in-situ temperature (SW_TEMP); (c) no-slip wall in MITgcm against a Cd u^2 drag in the theory; (c, comparison set-up) the ambient evolves by 36-48 h while the theory uses the initial profile; (b/c, small) MITgcm adds melt as a virtual salt flux with no volume, so the meltwater volume term in the plume volume budget is missing. A resolution and sensitivity study is pending (exp1_fjord_plumes/notes/pd2_convergence.md).
