# pyOCEAN cross-check of the (c) open boundary

In MITgcm, the (c) fjord runs with shelf forcing diverge under the Stevens eastern open boundary (`c_fjord_shelf` at day 12.8, `c_fjord_all` at day 20.3; `notes/pd1_c_fjord3d.md`). This directory rebuilds the same fjord in pyOCEAN (`~/pyOCEAN`, imported read-only), with a different open-boundary scheme: Flather (implicit θ form) on the depth mean, Orlanski plus nudging on the baroclinic velocity, and a 10-cell sponge (MITgcm OBCS profile, 1200/60 s) that relaxes u′, T and S toward the time-varying shelf exterior. The question is whether the instability belongs to MITgcm's open boundary or to the experiment design. The configuration and the four criteria were pre-registered, and approved by Esteban, before any run: `notes/pd1_c_fjord3d.md`, last section. `run_pyo_c.py --case {pyo_shelf,pyo_shelf_runoff} --days 30` builds and runs one case. It uses the (c) grid (200 × 30 cells of 200 m, side-wall rows land, 50 × 10 m levels), f0, the Sermilik profile, the linear EOS, Ah = Kh = 30 and Ar = Kr = 1e-5 m² s⁻¹, and the shelf OB records that `gendata/make_case._c_forcing` writes for MITgcm (pycnocline at 200 − 30 sin(2πt/6 d) m). It runs ALE z* with Δt = 60 s. `pyo_shelf_runoff` adds Q = 100 m³ s⁻¹ of fresh surface runoff in the six (c) plume cells at the fjord head. This is an estuarine analogue of the (c) plume, because pyOCEAN has no source at depth: a declared class-(b) difference. Daily snapshots, hourly series and `status.json` go to `/Volumes/LaCie/portfolio_runs/exp1/crosscheck_pyo/<case>/`. `eval_pyo_c.py` applies the criteria mechanically and writes `figures/crosscheck_pyo_c.json`. Its docstring fixes the operational choices the note leaves open: "at all times" means every step; "drift" means the maximum of |η̄(t) − η̄(20 d)| over days 20–30; the pycnocline is the 1.25 °C isotherm. Other numerical choices, each a class-(c) difference from MITgcm (c):
- pyOCEAN's open face is the domain edge (u-face 200), so the (c) land column 199 is wet here; the "3 columns next to the OB" are u-faces 198–200 (MITgcm: 196–198);
- the (c) quadratic bottom drag (2.5e-3) acts on the bottom 10 m;
- there is no convective adjustment (MITgcm uses `ivdc_kappa` = 10);
- tracer advection is WENO5 + FCT, not DST3 (scheme 33);
- K_h acts on T and S along the z* layers.
